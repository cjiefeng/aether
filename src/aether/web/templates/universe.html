    Universe review

    Once a month (the 1st, 10:00 SGT) Aether applies the same test to every current pure-play and every discovered candidate: a NYSE/Nasdaq listing with SEC filings, quantum as the principal business (from the T1 business section of its latest filing), market cap ≥ $500M and 20-session median dollar volume ≥ $5M, and at least 60 sessions of history (thresholds in config/universe.yaml). The model proposes; code overrides anything the criteria don't support. These are proposals only: apply one by editing watchlist.yaml in a PR. Evidence text is untrusted (from filings and web search).
